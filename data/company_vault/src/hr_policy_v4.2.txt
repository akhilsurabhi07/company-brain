SECTION 1 — EMPLOYMENT POLICIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1 WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, Monday through Friday.
Employees in engineering roles follow a flexible schedule (core hours: 11 AM–4 PM IST).
Remote employees must maintain availability during core hours.

1.2 PROBATION PERIOD
All new employees undergo a 90-day probation period.
Performance reviews occur at Day 30, Day 60, and Day 90.
Permanent employment requires satisfactory reviews at all three checkpoints.

1.3 ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENTS
- Full-time employees: 24 paid leave days per calendar year.
- Sick leave: 12 days per year (not carry-forward eligible).
- Casual leave: 6 days per year.
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks (as per Maternity Benefit Act, 2017).
- Paternity leave: 10 days.
- Bereavement leave: 5 days for immediate family members.

1.4 COMPENSATION REVIEW CYCLE
Salary reviews are conducted annually in April.
Merit increases range from 0% to 18% based on performance rating.
Performance ratings: Exceptional (5), Exceeds Expectations (4), Meets Expectations (3), Below Expectations (2), Unsatisfactory (1).
Employees rated 4 or above for two consecutive years are fast-tracked for promotion.

SECTION 2 — CODE OF CONDUCT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 ANTI-HARASSMENT POLICY
Company Brain Technologies has zero tolerance for any form of harassment.
All complaints must be filed with the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) within 90 days of the incident.
The ICC comprises: Presiding Officer (VP HR), two employee representatives, one external member.
Investigations must be completed within 60 days. Appeals may be filed within 30 days of the ICC's decision.

2.2 CONFLICT OF INTEREST
Employees must disclose any financial interest exceeding ₹50,000 in a competitor or vendor.
Board approval is required for moonlighting in a related industry.
Violations result in immediate termination without severance.

2.3 DATA SECURITY OBLIGATIONS
Each employee must complete the annual security awareness training (CSAT course, 4 hours).
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption (BitLocker / FileVault).
Personal devices accessing company systems must enroll in MDM within 7 days of onboarding.
Sharing credentials with any person, including colleagues, is strictly prohibited.

SECTION 3 — BENEFITS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 HEALTH INSURANCE
All employees and immediate family members (spouse + up to 2 children) are covered under the Group Medical Insurance plan.
Insurer: Star Health & Allied Insurance.
Sum Insured: ₹5,00,000 per family per annum.
Pre-existing conditions are covered from Day 1.
Dental and vision: Covered up to ₹25,000 per year under the Flexi-Benefits wallet.

3.2 FLEXI-BENEFITS WALLET
Annual Flexi-Benefits wallet: ₹60,000 (taxable if unused by March 31).
Eligible categories: Books & Learning, Gym & Wellness, Gadgets (work-related), Broadband, Dental, Vision.
Reimbursement via Darwinbox portal — maximum claim turnaround: 7 working days.

3.3 EMPLOYEE STOCK OPTION PLAN (ESOP)
Vesting schedule: 4-year cliff-vesting with 25% vest at year 1 and monthly thereafter.
Strike price set at Series A valuation: ₹47 per share (as of March 2024).
ESOP pool size: 12% of fully diluted equity.
Exercise window after termination: 90 days.

3.4 LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT
Annual L&D budget: ₹1,20,000 per employee.
Eligible spends: Online courses (Coursera, Udemy, Pluralsight), certifications (AWS, GCP, Azure, CISSP), conference attendance.
Books: Up to ₹15,000 per year from L&D budget.
Study leave: Up to 5 days per year for certification exams.

SECTION 4 — PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 OKRS & GOAL SETTING
OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) are set quarterly.
OKR completion below 70% triggers a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
PIP duration: 60 days, with bi-weekly check-ins with direct manager and HR.
Three consecutive quarters below 70% OKR completion results in separation.

4.2 360° FEEDBACK
Annual 360° feedback is collected in November.
Participants: Direct manager, 2 peers (chosen by employee), 2 cross-functional collaborators (chosen by manager).
Results are shared with the employee but NOT the specific reviewers (to ensure anonymity).

SECTION 5 — SEPARATION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 RESIGNATION NOTICE PERIOD
- Below Manager level: 30 days.
- Manager to Director: 60 days.
- VP and above: 90 days.
Notice periods can be negotiated; buyout at daily CTC rate.

5.2 GRATUITY
Gratuity is payable after 5 years of continuous service.
Formula: (Last drawn basic salary × 15 × years of service) / 26.
Maximum gratuity payable: ₹20,00,000 (as per Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, 2018 amendment).

5.3 EXIT INTERVIEW
Mandatory exit interview with HR Business Partner.
Equipment return checklist must be completed before full-and-final (F&F) settlement.
F&F settlement timeline: 45 days from last working day.
